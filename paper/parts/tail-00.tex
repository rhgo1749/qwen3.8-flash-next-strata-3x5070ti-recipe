DeepSpeed-MoE established optimized distributed MoE inference~\cite{deepspeedmoe}. DWDP preserves independent data-parallel progress while distributing MoE weights across peer GPUs and fetching missing experts on demand~\cite{dwdp}. SiDP similarly treats weights as a bandwidth-backed shared resource, using remote-weight streaming or remote computation on datacenter GPUs~\cite{sidp}. vLLM-Omni Distributed Layerwise Offload uses mmap-backed checkpoint views to share OS page-cache pages across processes and can optionally reconstruct sharded layers with AllGather for diffusion workloads~\cite{vllmomni}. These are important precedents: neither data-parallel independence nor mmap/page-cache sharing is novel here. Our measured mechanism is specifically a cross-process shared backing for the pinned host expert arena of an autoregressive offload runtime, while each lane retains its own GPU hot cache, KV, speculation, and request state.

\paragraph{Serving memory management and consumer runtimes.}
vLLM's PagedAttention reduces KV-cache fragmentation and supports sharing at the request-memory level~\cite{vllm}. FreeToken explores MoE offloading and host-residency tradeoffs on consumer/workstation hardware~\cite{freetoken}. Our contribution is complementary and deliberately smaller: a replica-memory optimization and serving topology for one partially offloaded MoE runtime, not a general replacement for these engines.
